## 信息摘要

Rencontres de Moriond 2027 引力专题会议将于 2027 年 3 月 14–21 日在意大利阿尔卑斯山区的 La Thuile 举行。会议覆盖广泛的引力物理研究，包括引力波理论与数据分析、实验进展及探测器概念等；LISA 相关研究历来在该会议中占有较大比重。会议注册和资助申请截止日期均为 2027 年 1 月 17 日，摘要提交截止日期为 1 月 19 日。

## 完整通知原文

Hi everyone,

We would like to draw your attention to the next Rencontres de Moriond – Gravitation, which will take place in La Thuile, Italy, from March 14 to 21, 2027, a beautiful Alpine resort and a great setting for a week of scientific discussions! (see <https://moriond.in2p3.fr/2027/Gravitation/moriond-gravitation-2027.html> for more information).

The conference brings together researchers from across the broad field of gravitational physics, covering topics ranging from gravitational-wave theory and data analysis to experimental developments and detector concepts. LISA-related topics have traditionally been very well represented, making this an excellent opportunity to share recent progress, exchange ideas, and connect with colleagues from the LISA community and also from different communities. You can get an idea of the scientific programme from the previous edition: <https://moriond.in2p3.fr/2025/Gravitation/gravitation-agenda.html>

Important deadlines:

- registration: January 17th <https://moriond.in2p3.fr/2027/Registration/apply.html>
- abstract submission: January 19th <https://moriond.in2p3.fr/2027/Gravitation/gravitation-contributions.html>
- grant application: January 17th

We hope to see many of you there,

Antoine Petiteau, Peter Wolf and Aurelien Hees for the SOC of Moriond
