# LISA Spain Meeting 2026

[返回会议与学术交流列表](README.md)

- **时间：** 2026 年 11 月 11–12 日
- **附加活动：** 11 月 13 日上午可选专题环节
- **地点：** Institute of Applied Computing and Community Code（IAC3），University of the Balearic Islands（UIB），西班牙马略卡岛帕尔马
- **注册及摘要截止：** 2026 年 10 月 29 日
- **会议网站：** <https://indico.global/event/18642/overview>
- **注册费：** 无；会议期间提供午餐和茶歇

## 信息摘要

LISA Spain Meeting 2026 将汇集西班牙 LISA 社群，交流 LISA 科学与技术的最新进展并讨论任务准备工作。议程包括邀请报告、投稿报告、海报及专题讨论，主题涵盖波形和源建模、天体物理、数据分析与搜索、模拟及仪器等。11 月 13 日上午另设可选专题环节，讨论西班牙 LISA 战略和 Data Distributed Processing Centre 相关活动。

## 完整通知原文

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that registration and abstract submission are now open for the LISA Spain Meeting 2026, which will take place on 11–12 November 2026 at the Institute of Applied Computing and Community Code (IAC3), University of the Balearic Islands (UIB), in Palma de Mallorca, Spain.

The meeting will bring together members of the Spanish LISA community to present and discuss recent scientific developments across the different areas of LISA science and technology, strengthen interactions between groups, and exchange ideas on future activities in preparation for the mission.

The scientific programme will include invited and contributed talks, poster presentations, and dedicated discussion sessions. We welcome contributions from across the LISA community, including waveform and source modelling, astrophysics, data analysis and searches, simulations, instrumentation, and other topics relevant to LISA. We particularly encourage early-career researchers to submit contributions and present their work.

In addition to the two main meeting days, we will hold an optional focused session on the morning of Friday, 13 November, with hands-on activities and discussions on topics of particular interest to the Spanish community, including LISA strategies in Spain and activities connected to the Data Distributed Processing Centre (DDPC).

Registration and abstract submission will remain open until 29 October 2026.

Further information, registration, and abstract submission are available on the meeting website:

<https://indico.global/event/18642/overview>

There is no registration fee. Lunches and coffee breaks during the meeting will be provided to participants.

We would be grateful if you could circulate this announcement among colleagues who may be interested.

We look forward to welcoming you to Palma de Mallorca in November!

With best regards,

The LISA Spain Meeting 2026 Organizing Committee
