## 完整通知原文

Dear colleagues,

The Department of Physics of the University of Pisa, the INFN, and the Vanderbilt Lunar Labs Initiative cordially invite the scientific community to the 2026 International LILA Meeting (Lunar Gravitational-Wave Workshop), taking place from 2 to 4 December 2026 in Pisa, Italy:

<https://agenda.infn.it/event/53122/>

As the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA network continues to detect gravitational-wave signals at an unprecedented rate, plans for next-generation detectors are rapidly advancing, including ground-based observatories like the Einstein Telescope and Cosmic Explorer as well as the spaceborne mission LISA. In the meantime, novel proposals for lunar-based detectors are being developed to open the mid-band frequency range (0.1–10 Hz), bridging the gap between terrestrial and space detectors. With its extreme seismic quietness and natural ultra-high vacuum, the Moon provides an ideal environment for a gravitational-wave observatory. The Laser Interferometer Lunar Antenna (LILA) is an international and interdisciplinary initiative with the goal of developing a mid-band detector based on the technologies of current and future GW interferometers.

This is the sixth annual meeting in the Lunar GW workshop series, following editions at Johns Hopkins APL (2025), Belle-Île-en-Mer (2024), Nashville (2023), Bern (2022), and Cascina (2021). The 2026 edition will be held at the Museum of Physics Instruments of the University of Pisa.

Key scientific session topics will include:

- Mid-band gravitational-wave science
- Multi-messenger astrophysics
- GW instrumentation and technology development for lunar detectors
- Lunar geoscience
