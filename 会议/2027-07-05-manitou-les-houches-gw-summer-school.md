# 2027 MaNiTou 引力波暑期学校（Les Houches）

[返回会议与学术交流列表](README.md)

- **时间：** 2027 年 7 月 5–30 日
- **地点：** Les Houches School of Physics，法国 Les Houches
- **对象：** 优先面向博士生、青年博士后和优秀硕士生；也考虑计划转入引力波研究的科研人员、教师和工程师
- **申请与注册：** 尚未开放，后续公布
- **暂定费用：** 2100 欧元，包含住宿和餐饮；组委会正在筹集经费以降低费用并提供部分奖学金
- **咨询联系：** Nelson Christensen（<nelson.christensen@oca.eu>）或其他组委会成员

## 信息摘要

2027 年 MaNiTou 引力波暑期学校将在法国 Les Houches 举办，为期四周。课程覆盖引力波理论与数据分析、波形建模、数值相对论、地面和空间引力波探测器、LISA 科学、脉冲星计时阵列、引力波宇宙学、多信使观测、贝叶斯参数估计及人工智能应用等主题。申请与注册细节尚未公布。

## 完整通知原文

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that the 2027 session of the MaNiTou summer school on gravitational waves will be at the Les Houches School of Physics (Les Houches, France) in 2027. This will be a month long summer school, from July 5 to July 30. So please mark your schedules.

Application and registration information will be coming soon. The priority for the school will be PhD students, young postdocs, and exceptional masters students. We will also consider participation from researchers/professors/engineers thinking to change their research area to gravitational waves.

The nominal cost for the school will be 2100 €; this will cover accommodation and meals. The organizing committee is actively trying to raise funds to diminish this cost and provide some scholarships. Again, more information will be coming soon.

The confirmed speakers and topics are the following:

- Simone Speziale, gravitational-wave theory
- Frédérique Marion, gravitational-wave data analysis
- Joe Romano, physical conditions to create gravitational waves, and stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds
- Sylvain Marsat, hands-on sessions on gravitational-wave data analysis
- Walid Chaibi, ground-based gravitational-wave interferometers
- Alessandra Buonanno, gravitational waveform modeling
- Michela Mapelli, the astrophysics of gravitational-wave sources, and gravitational-wave and binary systems: formation and astrophysics
- Samaya Nissanke, lectures on electromagnetic followup of gravitational-wave events: techniques and results
- Neil Cornish, an introduction to Bayesian parameter estimation, and applications to gravitational-wave sources
- Mairi Sakellariadou, early universe cosmology and beyond the standard model particle physics, observational cosmology with gravitational waves, and tests of theories of gravity with gravitational-waves
- Simone Mastrogiovanni, cosmology with gravitational waves, Bayesian methods, technical aspects, and future studies with third generation gravitational-wave detectors
- Viola Sordini, recent observations by LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA and their implications
- Stefan Hild, advanced quantum techniques and third-generation gravitational-wave interferometers
- Eric Gourgoulhon, black hole physics
- Gudrun Wanner, space based gravitational-wave detectors - LISA and future missions
- Harald Pfeiffer, numerical relativity: introduction and application to black holes
- Tim Dietritch, numerical relativity: binaries with neutron stars
- Astrid Lamberts, LISA science and sources
- Natalia Korsakova, an introduction to artificial intelligence techniques with applications for gravitational waves - tools for coding and data analysis, including hands-on sessions
- Maria Alessandra Papa, continuous wave gravitational-wave searches
- Michael Kramer, pulsars, pulsar observations, and pulsar timing
- Bruce Allen, pulsar timing arrays: introduction and detection principles, and PTA harmonic analysis
- Michele Vallisneri, the Bayesian approach for PTA, results

For any immediate questions, please reach out to a member of the Organizing Committee.

We look forward to seeing you in Les Houches!
