## 完整通知原文

Poincaré early-career researchers fellowship

Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur, Nice, France

The Henri Poincaré Early-Career Researcher Fellowship, funded by the Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur (OCA) and the Université Côte d'Azur IdEx JEDI, offers a two-year postdoctoral contract to early-career researchers who obtained their PhD and pursued their postdoctoral research outside OCA. The fellowship is intended to enable successful candidates to develop an independent research program in the broad field of Earth and Universe sciences. This covers gravitational-wave research. There are active research programs at OCA for Virgo, Einstein Telescope and LISA.

See <https://www.earthworks-jobs.com/jobpost/oca26091?id=11688> for more information and application instructions. The deadline for submitting an application is November 16, 2026.

For further questions, contact Nelson Christensen <nelson.christensen@oca.eu>
