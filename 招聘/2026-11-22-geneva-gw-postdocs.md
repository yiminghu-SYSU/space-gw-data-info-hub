## 完整通知原文

We are pleased to invite expressions of interest for two postdoctoral positions funded by the SNSF Starting Grant *“Towards Precision Astrophysics in the GW Sky”*.

The two postdoctoral researchers will join the group of Prof. Giulia Cusin at the Observatory of Geneva (Versoix, Geneva) and will pursue research in the following areas:

- **Gravitational-wave background forecasts and mapping with PTAs**, with a focus on multimessenger approaches and on constraining the properties of the underlying source populations;
- **Modelling of environmental and lensing effects**, and their impact on GW observations and astrophysical inference.

One of the positions will have a stronger **data-analysis focus**, while the other will be more **modelling-oriented**. We are looking for motivated and dynamic researchers with a strong background in gravitational-wave physics. However, strong researchers from other fields who are highly motivated and eager to learn are also encouraged to apply.

The successful candidates will join a dynamic and friendly research environment at the Observatory of Geneva, with research activities spanning a broad range of topics in astrophysics and cosmology. The Geneva area also hosts a large and active gravitational-wave community, offering ample opportunities for collaboration and interaction across different research groups.

The positions are expected to start in **autumn 2027**, although earlier or later starting dates can also be considered. One position is for 2 years, the other for 2+1 years.

The deadline for applications is **November 22**.

Applicants should hold a PhD in physics, astronomy, or a related field, or expect to obtain their PhD before the start of the position.

Interested candidates can apply here:

<https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo/jobs/32750>

We would greatly appreciate it if you could forward this announcement to any potentially interested candidates.

Giulia Cusin
