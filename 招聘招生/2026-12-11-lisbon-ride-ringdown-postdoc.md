## 信息摘要

里斯本 Instituto Superior Técnico 的 grit/CENTRA 招聘一名引力物理博士后，加入 Nicola Franchini 主持的 RiDE 项目。项目旨在精确计算黑洞等致密天体的准正规模及其激发，并研究广义相对论和超越广义相对论理论中的相关问题。具有黑洞物理或引力波物理背景者优先，尤其欢迎从事广义相对论检验、黑洞微扰理论、引力波建模或引力波数据分析的申请人。

## 完整通知原文

Dear all,

The Gravity Group at Tecnico (GRIT), part of the Center for Astrophysics and Gravitation (CENTRA) in Tecnico, Lisbon, invites expressions of interest for a postdoctoral position in gravitational physics.

The successful applicant will work with Dr. Nicola Franchini on the project “RingDown Excitation (RiDE)”, funded by the Fundacao para a Ciencia e Tecnologia (FCT) under the Exploratory Research Projects Program. The project’s main goal is to perform an accurate calculation of quasi-normal modes and their excitation in general relativity and beyond. The fellow will be expected to contribute to the project’s research goals and activities.

The appointment is for 1.5 years. The start date is strictly tied to the unlocking of the funds from the funding agency which can occur any time between February and April 2027.

Candidates must hold a PhD degree in Physics or related areas obtained at most 3 years before the time of start of the position. A strong background in black-hole and/or gravitational-wave physics is desirable. Candidates with experience in topics such as tests of GR, black-hole perturbation theory, gravitational-wave modelling and gravitational-wave data analysis, are strongly encouraged to apply.

Applications must be formalized by sending the following documents: i) a motivation letter; ii) CV (including list of publications); iii) a research statement (max 3 page); iv) PhD degree certificate or expected date of completion of the PhD; v) two recommendation letters to be sent separately.

Expressions of interest should be sent by e-mail to <nicola.franchini@tecnico.ulisboa.pt> [please use the subject: “Post-doctoral fellowship – 2025.21344.PTDC”]. The deadline for submitting expressions of interest and reference letters is December 11th, 2026. E-mails received after the deadline will not be considered.

Information about both the team and the broader CENTRA research unit to which grit belongs can be found at [CENTRA](https://centra.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/).

Informal inquiries can be made by contacting Nicola Franchini at `nicola.franchini[at]tecnico.ulisboa.pt`.

Best,  
Nicola
