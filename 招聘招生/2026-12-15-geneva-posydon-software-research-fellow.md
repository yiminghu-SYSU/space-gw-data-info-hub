## 完整通知原文

Dear colleagues,

I would like to advertise a four-year Software Research Fellow position in my group at the University of Geneva, contributing to the development of [POSYDON](https://posydon.org/), an open-source framework for modelling stellar and binary populations and the progenitors of gravitational-wave sources.

The position is part of SWISS-ET, supporting scientific preparations for the Einstein Telescope. Relevant development areas include connecting POSYDON with stellar- and gas-dynamics tools, improving stellar and binary-evolution modelling, and producing synthetic gravitational-wave source catalogues calibrated against current observations.

Scientific software development is the primary focus, including implementation, validation, testing and public releases within the international POSYDON collaboration. Up to 40% of working time may be allocated, by agreement, to independent and collaborative research using or extending POSYDON, aligned with SWISS-ET priorities.

We welcome applicants with a PhD in astrophysics, computational physics, scientific computing or a related field, strong Python skills and demonstrated contributions to scientific software. The appointment is at the Maître-assistant level, above a standard postdoctoral position, with greater scientific autonomy and responsibility.

Application deadline: 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

Full details and application instructions are available in the [AAS Job Register advertisement](https://aas.org/jobregister/ad/2b155440).

Please share this opportunity with interested colleagues and potential applicants. I would be happy to answer informal enquiries.

Best wishes,  
Tassos Fragos  
Department of Astronomy, University of Geneva
