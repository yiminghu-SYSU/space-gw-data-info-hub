## 信息摘要

里斯本 Instituto Superior Técnico 的 gravity group（grit）邀请潜在申请人表达参与即将开放的 Portuguese Science Foundation（FCT）研究职位申请的意向。课题组只能支持少量申请，有意者应在 2026 年 12 月前向 David Hilditch 发送简历、论文列表和研究陈述，以便在正式申请预计开放前进行准备。该资助提供三年职位，并根据申请人的经验设置不同级别；成功申请者预计于 2027 年夏季以后到岗，开始时间具有一定灵活性。

## 完整通知原文

Dear Colleagues,

The gravity group (grit) at Instituto Superior Técnico, Lisbon, invites potential candidates to express their interest in the upcoming Portuguese Science Foundation (FCT) grant opening.

The group can support a small number of applications, and we therefore ask interested parties to send a CV, publication list and research statement to David Hilditch (david.hilditch@tecnico.ulisboa.pt) with the subject “FCT-Application-2026” before December 2026, when the call itself is expected to open. See here for guidance on the previous iteration: <https://www.fct.pt/en/concursos/concurso-estimulo-ao-emprego-cientifico-individual-8-edicao/>

The grant itself offers a three year position, with different levels available depending on experience. Successful applications would be expected to start after the summer 2027, with a good degree of flexibility.

Information about both the team and the broader CENTRA research unit to which grit belongs can be found here: <https://centra.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/>. For any additional queries, you can contact me (Richard Brito, richard.brito@tecnico.ulisboa.pt) or David Hilditch (david.hilditch@tecnico.ulisboa.pt) directly.

Best,

Richard Brito

> 正式申请尚未开放；请以 FCT 及课题组后续通知中的最新要求和日期为准。
