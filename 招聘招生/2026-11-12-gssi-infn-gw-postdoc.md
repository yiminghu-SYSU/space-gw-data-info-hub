## 申请资格提示

申请人须为从外国机构取得博士学位的非意大利公民，或连续在国外任职至少三年的意大利公民。博士学位原则上应在 2018 年 11 月 12 日或之后授予，特定情形可以延期；预计在入职前完成博士学位者也可以申请。此外，既往 INFN 任职经历还可能受到其他限制，请务必查阅正式招聘文件。

## 信息摘要

该职位由意大利国家核物理研究所（INFN）资助，入选者将加入 GSSI Gravity Group 和 INFN TEONGRAV 研究网络，从事面向 LISA 的源建模、族群分析和系统误差等研究。团队研究覆盖引力物理、相对论天体物理、致密天体及引力波数据分析。职位主题为“Implications for Fundamental Physics from LISA Observations: Source Modelling, Population Analyses, and Systematic Effects”。

## 完整通知原文

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce an opening for a postdoctoral fellowship in gravitational-wave physics, funded by the Italian National Institute for Nuclear Physics (INFN), within the 2027–2028 INFN Fellowship Programme.

The successful candidate will join the Gravity Group at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) in L’Aquila, Italy, and become part of the INFN TEONGRAV research network.

The group offers a collaborative and multidisciplinary research environment, bringing together expertise in gravitational physics, relativistic astrophysics, compact objects, and gravitational-wave data analysis.

We welcome applications from candidates with expertise in gravitational-wave astrophysics, particularly in source and waveform modelling, gravitational self-force, astrophysical population studies, and gravitational-wave data analysis. Experience with extreme- and intermediate-mass-ratio inspirals, environmental effects, or tests of General Relativity would be particularly relevant.

The fellowship is initially offered for two years, with the possibility of renewal for an additional year (2+1 years), subject to funding availability and approval.

The annual gross salary is €36,000. The successful candidate is expected to start no later than 1 November 2027, although requests for a deferred starting date may be considered.

Applicants must be either non-Italian citizens with a PhD obtained from a foreign institution, or Italian citizens who have held a position abroad continuously for at least three years. The PhD must have been awarded on or after 12 November 2018, although extensions are possible under specific circumstances. Candidates expecting to complete their PhD before the start of the fellowship are also eligible. Additional eligibility restrictions concerning previous INFN appointments apply; please refer to the official call for details.

Applications must be submitted through the INFN recruitment portal: <https://reclutamento.dsi.infn.it/>  
Please look for Call No. 29216, entitled “POST-DOCTORAL FELLOWSHIPS IN THEORETICAL PHYSICS”.

Candidates should select Position 8 (INFN National Laboratories of Gran Sasso), entitled: “Implications for Fundamental Physics from LISA Observations: Source Modelling, Population Analyses, and Systematic Effects”

Applications should include a CV, publication list, and the names and email addresses of three referees.  
Application deadline: 12 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

We would appreciate it if you could circulate this announcement among potentially interested candidates.

Best regards,

Andrea
