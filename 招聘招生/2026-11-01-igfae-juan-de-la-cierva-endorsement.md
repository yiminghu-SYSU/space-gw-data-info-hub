# IGFAE：Juan de la Cierva 2026 博士后 Fellowship 推荐支持

[返回招聘与招生列表](README.md)

- **接收机构：** Instituto Galego de Física de Altas Enerxías（IGFAE），Universidade de Santiago de Compostela，西班牙
- **机会类型：** 申请 Juan de la Cierva 2026 博士后 Fellowship 的接收机构推荐支持（endorsement）
- **研究方向：** 理论天体物理与宇宙学，包括星系与恒星形成、引力波、CMB 与大尺度结构、暗物质和粒子宇宙学等
- **IGFAE 意向截止：** 2026 年 11 月 1 日
- **聘期：** 3 年全职合同
- **待遇：** 税前年薪 31,200 欧元；整个资助期另有 15,000 欧元用于科研流动和学术活动
- **申请材料：** 两页简历；一页说明，其中包括拟在 IGFAE 开展的简要研究计划及本人符合申请资格的说明
- **提交入口：** <https://academicprogramsonline.org/ajo/fellowship/33108>
- **官方 Fellowship 信息：** <https://www.aei.gob.es/en/announcements/announcements-finder/ayudas-contratos-juan-cierva-2026>
- **咨询联系：** Gonzalo Alonso Álvarez（<gonzalo.alonso@usc.es>）

## 申请资格提示

申请人须在 2024 年 1 月 1 日至 2026 年 12 月 31 日之间取得博士学位。因职业中断等情况可能获得期限延展，完整资格要求须以 Juan de la Cierva 2026 官方通知为准。IGFAE 预计至少推荐一名申请人；入围者将在西班牙国家研究署（AEI）的正式截止日期前获联系并进一步讨论申请。

## 信息摘要

IGFAE 理论天体物理与宇宙学组邀请有意申请 Juan de la Cierva 2026 Fellowship 的研究人员提交材料，由 IGFAE 作为接收机构提供推荐支持。该组成员包括 Marta Reina Campos、Juan Calderón Bustillo、Antón Baleato Lizancos 和 Gonzalo Alonso Álvarez，研究覆盖星系与恒星形成、引力波、宇宙微波背景与大尺度结构、暗物质及粒子宇宙学。理论天体物理或宇宙学各方向的优秀申请人均可申请。

Postdoc opportunity in Spain

Endorsement for the postdoctoral Fellowship Juan de la Cierva 2026

**Centro/Institution:** Instituto Galego de Física de Altas Enerxías  
**Tipo/Type:** Postdoctoral  
**País/Country:** Spain  
**Fecha límite:** 2026-11-01

**Descripción/Description:** Endorsement for the postdoctoral Fellowship Juan de la Cierva 2026

Instituto Galego de Física de Altas Enerxías (IGFAE), Universidade de Santiago de Compostela, Spain

The Theoretical Astrophysics and Cosmology group at IGFAE invites expressions of interest from candidates wishing to apply to the 2026 Juan de la Cierva (JDC) fellowship call (<https://www.aei.gob.es/en/announcements/announcements-finder/ayudas-contratos-juan-cierva-2026>) with IGFAE as the host institution.

IGFAE (<https://igfae.usc.es>) is a joint centre of the Universidade de Santiago de Compostela and Xunta de Galicia. Faculty members in the theoretical astrophysics and cosmology group include Marta Reina Campos, Juan Calderón Bustillo, Antón Baleato Lizancos, and Gonzalo Alonso Álvarez, with active research programmes in galaxy and star formation, gravitational waves, CMB & large-scale structure, dark matter and particle cosmology. Successful fellows will benefit from the stimulating research environment at IGFAE spanning particle physics, nuclear physics, high-energy astrophysics and quantum science and technology.

The fellowship provides a 3-year full-time employment contract, with a gross salary €31,200/year plus an additional €15,000 over the grant period for research mobility and activities. We expect to endorse at least one candidate; applications from outstanding researchers in any area of theoretical astrophysics or cosmology will be considered.

**Eligibility:** Applicants must hold a PhD obtained between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2026 (extensions possible for career interruptions; see the official call to verify full eligibility requirements).

To be considered for our endorsement, please send the following materials by Nov 1 through AcademicJobsOnline (<https://academicprogramsonline.org/ajo/fellowship/33108>):

- A 2-page CV
- A one-page document covering: (1) a brief research proposal describing the work you would pursue at IGFAE, and (2) a statement of your eligibility for the call

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for further discussion before the AEI deadline. We particularly encourage applications from underrepresented groups in physics.
