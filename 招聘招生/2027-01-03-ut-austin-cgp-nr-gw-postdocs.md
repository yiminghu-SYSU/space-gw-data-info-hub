## 信息摘要

The University of Texas at Austin 的 Center for Gravitational Physics 招聘最多两名引力波物理博士后，计划于 2027 年 9 月入职。招聘重点包括数值相对论、引力波源与波形建模、引力波数据分析、参数估计、观测解释，以及计算和机器学习方法。入选者可与中心教师及更广泛的引力波研究群体开展合作。CGP 教师包括 Hsin-Yu Chen、Pablo Laguna、Richard Matzner、Deirdre Shoemaker、Jay Wadekar 和 Aaron Zimmerman。

## 完整通知原文

Dear Colleagues,

We encourage applications to up to 2 postdoctoral fellowship positions in gravitational wave physics at UT Austin.

The Center for Gravitational Physics (CGP) at The University of Texas at Austin invites applications for up to two postdoctoral positions beginning in September 2027.

We are particularly interested in candidates working in numerical relativity, gravitational-wave modeling, and gravitational-wave data analysis. Areas of interest include numerical simulations of compact-object systems, gravitational-wave source modeling and waveform development, parameter estimation and interpretation of gravitational-wave observations, and applications of computational and machine-learning methods to gravitational-wave science.

CGP faculty include Hsin-Yu Chen, Pablo Laguna, Richard Matzner, Deirdre Shoemaker, Jay Wadekar, and Aaron Zimmerman. Successful candidates will have opportunities to collaborate broadly across the Center and the gravitational-wave community.

Application deadline: January 3, 2027
