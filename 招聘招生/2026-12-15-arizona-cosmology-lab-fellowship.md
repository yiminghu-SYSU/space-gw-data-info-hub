# University of Arizona：ACL 宇宙学与引力波天体物理 Fellowship

[返回招聘与招生列表](README.md)

- **机构：** Arizona Cosmology Lab（ACL），University of Arizona
- **职位：** ACL Postdoctoral Fellowship，预计招聘多人
- **研究方向：** 宇宙学与引力波天体物理
- **重点方向：** 低频引力波天体物理，包括理论与数据分析，以及超大质量/大质量黑洞天体物理和引力波观测之间的联系
- **支持条件：** 优先使用计算资源；由资深教师提供密切指导；可共同指导研究生并指导本科生
- **申请材料：** 求职信、简历、研究陈述、论文列表及三封推荐信
- **申请截止：** 2026 年 12 月 15 日
- **申请链接：** <https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo/jobs/32884>
- **低频引力波方向咨询：** Gabriela Sato-Polito（<gsatopolito@arizona.edu>）

## 信息摘要

University of Arizona 的 Arizona Cosmology Lab 公开招聘多名 ACL 博士后，研究领域覆盖宇宙学和引力波天体物理。低频引力波天体物理是优先方向之一，涉及理论、数据分析以及超大质量或大质量黑洞与引力波观测之间的联系。入选者可优先使用计算资源，获得资深教师指导，并参与研究生共同指导和本科生科研指导。

## 完整通知原文

FYI

The Arizona Cosmology Lab (ACL) at the University of Arizona, invites applications for the ACL Fellowship, with multiple hires expected in cosmology and gravitational-wave astrophysics. One of the priority areas is low-frequency gravitational-wave astrophysics, spanning theory and data analysis, including the connection between supermassive/massive black hole astrophysics and gravitational-wave observations. Fellows receive priority access to computational resources, close mentorship by experienced faculty, and opportunities to co-advise graduate students and mentor undergraduates. Applications (cover letter, CV, research statement, publication list, and three reference letters) should be submitted on AJO at <https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo/jobs/32884> by December 15, 2026. Questions about the low-frequency GW area can be directed to Gabriela Sato-Polito at <gsatopolito@arizona.edu>.
