## 信息摘要

AEI Potsdam 的 IMPRS “Gravity at the Extreme” 招收多个全额资助博士生。入选者将加入 AEI 的 Astrophysical and Cosmological Relativity、Computational Relativistic Astrophysics，或 AEI 与 University of Potsdam 联合开展的 Multi-messenger Astrophysics of Compact Binaries / Theoretical Astrophysics 研究团队。研究方向覆盖解析相对论与引力波源建模、数值相对论、引力波数据分析、基础物理与宇宙学，以及多信使和高能天体物理。项目免学费，并为会议参与和职业发展提供支持。

## 研究方向

- **解析相对论与引力波源建模：** 后牛顿和后闵可夫斯基理论、引力自力、黑洞微扰理论、有效单体方法及超越广义相对论的研究；
- **数值相对论：** 广义相对论及替代引力理论中的致密天体模拟，恒星坍缩形成黑洞和中子星；
- **引力波数据分析：** LIGO、Virgo 和 KAGRA 数据，LISA 和 Einstein Telescope 的数据分析流程与方法，以及机器学习等加速推断技术；
- **基础物理、天体物理与宇宙学：** 强场和高度动态条件下的引力检验、致密双星族群、环境效应，以及暗能量、暗物质和引力透镜等引力波宇宙学研究；
- **多信使与高能天体物理：** 中子星并合、电磁对应体、中子星状态方程、核合成及伽马射线暴。

## 完整通知原文

Dear all,

The AEI in Potsdam invites applications for several fully funded PhD positions in the International Max Planck Research School (IMPRS) “Gravity at the Extreme”. We are looking for creative, proactive, and motivated candidates who thrive in a vibrant, interdisciplinary team.

### WHERE YOU WILL WORK

You will join one of these research units in Potsdam:

- Astrophysical and Cosmological Relativity department (ACR), AEI
- Computational Relativistic Astrophysics department (CRA), AEI
- Multi-messenger Astrophysics of Compact Binaries group, AEI / Theoretical Astrophysics group, University of Potsdam (UP)

The IMPRS connects the AEI (Potsdam and Hannover) with the University of Potsdam, Humboldt University Berlin, and Leibniz University Hannover, as well as international affiliates at the University of Maryland and the Yukawa Institute for Theoretical Physics (Kyoto). Students can join the LIGO Scientific Collaboration (soon International Gravitational-Wave Observatory Network (IGWN)) (via ACR), the Distributed Data Processing Center (DDPC) (via ACR), the LISA Consortium (via ACR), and the Einstein Telescope Collaboration (via AEI–UP). ACR and CRA run three in-house high-performance compute clusters for source modeling, waveform development, and GW data analysis.

### WHAT YOU COULD WORK ON

- Analytical relativity and source modeling: gravitational dynamics and radiation with post-Newtonian and post-Minkowskian theory, gravitational self-force, black-hole perturbation theory, and effective-one-body formalism in general relativity and beyond
- Numerical relativity: simulations of compact objects in general relativity and alternative theories of gravity; stellar collapse to black holes and neutron stars
- Gravitational-wave data analysis: LIGO, Virgo, and KAGRA data; pipelines and methods for LISA and the Einstein Telescope; accelerated inference, including machine learning
- Fundamental physics, astrophysics & cosmology: tests of gravity in the strong-field, highly dynamical regime; compact-binary populations, environmental effects, cosmography with gravitational waves (dark energy, dark matter, lensing)
- Multi-messenger & high-energy astrophysics: neutron-star mergers, electromagnetic counterparts, the neutron-star equation of state, nucleosynthesis, gamma-ray bursts

### WHAT WE OFFER

- Full funding with no tuition fees, plus support for conferences and professional development
- A structured, English-language graduate program with individual supervision and mentoring by research scientists who are leaders in their respective fields
- World-class facilities and cutting-edge research projects in a worldwide network
- Practical support: computing facilities, PhD representatives, language courses, visa and residence permit help, childcare and family services – just 30 minutes from central Berlin

### REQUIREMENTS

A Master’s degree (or equivalent) by the start of the PhD. Research is carried out at the AEI in Potsdam; the doctorate is awarded by the University of Potsdam or Humboldt University Berlin.
