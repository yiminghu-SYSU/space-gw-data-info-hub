# Syracuse University OSPO：Research Software Engineer 博士后（多个）

[返回招聘与招生列表](README.md)

- **机构：** Syracuse University Open Source Program Office（SU-OSPO）
- **职位：** 一名或多名博士后 Research Software Engineer（RSE）
- **合作负责人：** Collin Capano、Duncan Brown
- **主要工作：** 支持和开发校内开源软件、科研软件工程、开源教育与学生指导，以及开放权重的大语言模型相关研究
- **申请审议：** 2026 年 12 月 7 日开始，招满为止
- **申请页面：** <https://www.sujobopps.com/postings/115010>

## 信息摘要

Syracuse University Open Source Program Office 招聘一名或多名博士后，以 Research Software Engineer 身份支持校内科研和大学运营所需的开源软件开发，并进入学术界 RSE 职业发展路径。岗位工作包括帮助教职员工建设和维护科研软件、开发开源教育材料、指导学生软件开发、参与开放权重大语言模型研究，以及跟踪校内开源软件包的使用情况。入选者也受到鼓励继续开展本人专业领域的研究。

## 主要职责

- 帮助教职员工开发和维护用于科研及大学运营的软件，重点包括开源 Claude skills 的开发；
- 为学校社区开发开源软件相关的教育材料；
- 为学生提供软件开发指导；
- 参与或负责开放权重大语言模型的使用研究；
- 跟踪校内开源软件包的使用情况；
- 关注并推广开源社区的最佳实践。

## 申请提示

该岗位不要求提交研究陈述。申请人应在求职信中说明软件工程经验，并重点介绍参与过的开源开发工作。完整申请要求请以职位页面为准。

## 完整通知原文

Dear all,

The [Syracuse University Open Source Program Office](https://opensource.syracuse.edu/) (SU-OSPO) invites applications for one or more postdocs to work as research software engineers (RSEs) and to help support and develop open-source software on campus.

This position will put candidates on an RSE career path within academia, for which there are many opportunities. For example, the current OSPO postdoc obtained a permanent RSE position at UMass Amherst.

The qualified candidates will assist OSPO Director Collin Capano and Vice President of Research Duncan Brown to:

- help faculty and staff build and maintain software for research and university operations, with a focus on developing open-source Claude skills;
- develop educational materials for the university community related to open-source development;
- provide software development mentorship to students;
- oversee research in the use of open-weight LLMs;
- track the use of open-source packages on campus;
- stay abreast of best practices in the open-source community.

The candidates are also encouraged to pursue their own research in their domain of expertise.

If you or someone you know is interested in applying, please see the job ad at: <https://www.sujobopps.com/postings/115010>.

Note that we do not require a research statement. Instead, we ask that applicants indicate their software engineering experience in their cover letter, highlighting any open-source development they have done.

Review of applications will begin on 12/7/26 and continue until the position(s) are filled.

Please forward to anyone you think might be interested!

Best,  
Collin
